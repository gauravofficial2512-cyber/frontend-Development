Our Vision

Our vision is to be the most beloved and trusted dining
institution of Vrindavan and Mathura — a place where the
soul of Braj's culinary tradition meets contemporary refinement.
We envision a world where every guest, from the devotee on a sacred
pilgrimage to the family on a weekend getaway, leaves our door nourished,
joyful, and transformed.

Our Managing Director


Jagdeesh Nauhwar built Rudr Shiva dhaba By Jagdeesh
